BS POKER

Declare a poker hand you claim exists in the shared pool. The next player declares higher — or calls BS. Six wild cards in the deck (four 2s + two jokers). Hit 6 cards → eliminated.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal

Stay in the game. Hit the card cap (default 6) → eliminated. Last player standing wins.

Each round

  • Everyone gets a hand. All hands together = the communal pool.
  • Starting player declares a poker hand they claim exists somewhere in that pool.
  • Next player either declares a strictly higher hand, or calls BS.
  • On BS: cards reveal. If the declared hand exists → caller takes +1 card. If it doesn't → declarer takes +1 card.
  • The loser of the round starts the next one.

Wild cards

Six wilds in the deck: all four 2s + two jokers. They count as any rank/suit.

Strategy hints

  • ~Pair almost always exists (96%) — too cheap to declare safely past round 1.
  • The bluff zone starts around straight / full house — that's where calls become a coin flip.
  • As more cards enter the pool, even quads becomes likely. Stop calling BS on big hands late.
  • If a CPU jumps straight to quads or straight flush early — they're usually bluffing.
